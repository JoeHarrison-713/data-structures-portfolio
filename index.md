# Joe Harrison

## About Me
My name is Joseph Harrison, and I am currently a Data Science major attending UNC Charlotte with a planned graduation of Spring 2028. I previously earned an Associate’s degree in International Business from Forsyth Technical Community College, which gave me a strong understanding of business fundamentals, markets, and organizational decision‑making. Now, I’m focused on learning the analytical side — how data drives those decisions. With a 3.5 GPA and growing experience in Python and statistical modeling, I’m working to build a real‑world portfolio that demonstrates both technical development and practical application. My goal is to create projects that show how data can be used to understand patterns, support decisions, and communicate insights clearly. Before returning to school full‑time, I worked as an independent freelance contractor while completing my associate’s degree. That experience exposed me to markets, loans, official paperwork, contracts, and professional expectations, things I had never seriously encountered before entering this field. It helped me understand how the real world operates in terms of deadlines, requirements, laws, and guidelines, and it taught me how to carry myself professionally. Those lessons continue to shape how I approach my work today. My long‑term goal is to build a career in banking analytics in Charlotte or pursue sports analytics wherever opportunities arise. I’m especially interested in how financial institutions use data to evaluate risk, improve customer experience, and support strategic decision‑making. To prepare for that path, I’m developing projects that highlight analytical depth, technical skill, and strong communication, including geospatial dashboards, sports analytics research, and statistical modeling work. As I continue progressing through my degree and portfolio, my focus remains on becoming a reliable, thoughtful, and impact‑driven analyst, someone who brings clarity, curiosity, and professionalism to every project while continually improving and expanding my skill set.
